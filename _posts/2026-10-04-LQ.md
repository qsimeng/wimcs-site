A poster abstract writing support session for undergraduate and postgraduate students who want to patriciate BCSWomen Lovelace Colloquium 2027: University of Birmingham. An annual event specifically for women and non-binary students studying computing in the UK.
 
Students whose poster are accepted will be invited to attend the event with their travel expenses covered and have the chance to win a prize. This would be a great opportunity to build connections with renowned female academics from the UK and gain valuable experience to enhance your CV, helping you secure a placement or job in the future.
