Schedule: 

Five academic sessions including AI and Machine Learning, Creative Coding, Cyber Security and Digital Forensics, Data Science & Environmental Modelling, and Human Computer Interaction.

Non-academic session: Day tour at Swansea Bay.

---
Tutors: Undergraduate/Postgraduate female students from Computer Science Department.

Who can apply: 

Year 12 female students from all subject backgrounds interested in exploring Computer Science and university study. No prior knowledge of Computer Science is required, just curiosity, enthusiasm, and a willingness to explore new ideas!
